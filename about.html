For over two decades, Harrison & Associates has stood as a pillar of legal integrity, championing the cause of justice in India's highest courts.

Advocate James Harrison

A Senior Advocate designated by the High Court of Delhi, James Harrison has been at the forefront of constitutional and civil litigation for over 25 years. His practice spans landmark constitutional matters, complex commercial disputes, and public interest litigation that has shaped legal precedent.

After graduating with honors from the National Law School of India University, Bangalore, Mr. Harrison began his practice under the tutelage of distinguished jurists. His incisive legal acumen and unwavering commitment to ethical practice quickly established him as a formidable presence in the courtroom.

Today, Harrison & Associates represents a diverse clientele including Fortune 500 corporations, government bodies, and individuals seeking justice. The firm's reputation rests on meticulous preparation, innovative legal strategies, and an uncompromising commitment to client success.
